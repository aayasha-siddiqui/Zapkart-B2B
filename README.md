# 📌 Project Overview

**Zapkart-B2B** is a powerful and scalable grocery ecommerce platform designed for:

- 🛒 Online Grocery Shopping
- 🏪 Multi Vendor Marketplace
- 🚚 Delivery Management
- 📦 Warehouse & Inventory System
- 👨‍💼 Seller & Shopkeeper Management
- 💳 Secure Online Payments
- 📍 Live Delivery Tracking
- 📧 Smart Email Notifications
- 🔐 OTP Delivery Verification
- 📊 Analytics & Monitoring

The platform creates a complete business ecosystem where:

✅ Customers can purchase products online  
✅ Sellers can manage products & inventory  
✅ Warehouse manages stock operations  
✅ Delivery partners can deliver orders efficiently  
✅ Admin controls and monitors the complete platform
